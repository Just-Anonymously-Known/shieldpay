ShieldPay

Live demo

This is a demo on Zcash testnet. No real money moves. The payment QR is real, but confirming it is simulated with one button, so you can walk through the privacy feature without a wallet. Everything after that (blind signing, wallet, redeeming, double-spend refusal) is the real code.

1. Buy vouchers

Your browser makes secret codes and hides them before the shop signs them. The shop never sees them.

On the next page tap Simulate payment received. Your vouchers then appear in your wallet.

2. Spend one at the shop

Open your voucher wallet, tap Show code, copy it and paste it here. This box plays the shop's counter.

Try the same code twice: the second time must be refused. The shop can check a voucher is genuine, but cannot tell which purchase it came from.
